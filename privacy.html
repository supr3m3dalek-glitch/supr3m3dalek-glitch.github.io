Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 24, 2026

"Drowsy Whisker Uploader" is a private tool used only by the owner of the YouTube channel The Drowsy Whisker. It is not offered to the public.

What the app does

The app uses the YouTube API Services to upload videos, with their title, description and tags, to the owner's own YouTube channel.

What data it uses

  • It only accesses the channel owner's own YouTube account, with the owner's permission.
  • The login key (OAuth token) is stored only on the owner's own computer and is never shared.
  • It does not collect, store, sell or share any data about viewers or other people.

Google and YouTube

This app uses YouTube API Services. By using it you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service. See also the Google Privacy Policy. Access can be removed at any time at Google Account permissions.

Contact

Questions can be sent via the YouTube channel @TheDrowsyWhisker.
